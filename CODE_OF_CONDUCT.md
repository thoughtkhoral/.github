# Code of Conduct

ThoughtKhoral is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically
constructive community.

Participants must not engage in harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, or publication of private information. Give
feedback about ideas and evidence, not about a contributor's identity or
character.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict
participation when conduct violates this policy. Report conduct concerns
privately to an organization owner through GitHub; do not include sensitive
personal information in a public issue.
